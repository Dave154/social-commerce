# PAGE 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW

## 1.1 About JK Rentals

JK Rentals is a customer-focused car rental company established to provide convenient, reliable and professionally managed vehicle rental services for individuals, families, tourists, business professionals and corporate organizations.

The company operates on the principle that renting a vehicle should be simple, transparent and dependable. Customers should be able to choose a suitable vehicle, understand the total rental cost, complete the necessary documentation and receive their vehicle without unnecessary complications.

JK Rentals provides short-term, medium-term and long-term vehicle rental solutions. Customers can rent vehicles for personal transportation, business meetings, airport transfers, vacations, weddings, events, temporary transportation needs and corporate assignments.

Our fleet is designed to accommodate different budgets and transportation requirements, ranging from economical compact vehicles to premium SUVs and executive vehicles.

## 1.2 Our Business Philosophy

JK Rentals focuses on solving transportation problems rather than simply providing vehicles.

Our service model is built around five principles:

* Reliable vehicles
* Transparent pricing
* Convenient booking
* Responsive customer support
* Professional customer experience

We understand that transportation can be time-sensitive. A delayed vehicle, poorly maintained car or unclear rental agreement can create significant problems for a customer. Our operations therefore emphasize preventive maintenance, vehicle inspections and clear communication.

## 1.3 Who We Serve

JK Rentals serves:

* Individuals
* Families
* Tourists and visitors
* Business travelers
* Corporate organizations
* Event planners
* Wedding planners
* NGOs and development organizations
* Government contractors
* Real estate professionals
* Film and media production companies
* Travel agencies
* Small and medium-sized businesses

# PAGE 8: ACCIDENTS, EMERGENCIES AND LATE RETURNS

# 20. ACCIDENT PROCEDURE

If a customer is involved in an accident, the customer's first priority should be personal safety.

Where appropriate, the customer should:

1. Move to a safe location if it is safe and legally appropriate to do so.
2. Contact emergency services where necessary.
3. Contact JK Rentals as soon as possible.
4. Exchange required information with other involved parties.
5. Avoid admitting liability on behalf of JK Rentals.
6. Take photographs when it is safe and lawful.
7. Cooperate with authorities and insurance representatives.
8. Complete any required incident documentation.

Customers should never leave the scene of an accident where applicable law requires them to remain.

## 20.1 Unauthorized Repairs

Customers should contact JK Rentals before authorizing significant repairs.

Emergency repairs necessary to protect life, safety or the vehicle may be handled according to the circumstances and later reviewed by JK Rentals.

# 27. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### 1. How old must I be to rent a car?

The standard minimum age for self-drive rentals is 23 years. Certain vehicle categories may require a higher minimum age.

### 2. Do I need a driving licence?

Yes. Customers renting vehicles for self-drive must have a valid driving licence appropriate for the vehicle.

### 3. Can someone else drive my rental car?

Only drivers approved and registered with JK Rentals may operate the rental vehicle.

### 4. Is a security deposit required?

Yes, most self-drive rentals require a refundable security deposit.

### 5. When will I receive my deposit back?

Where there are no outstanding charges, deposits are generally released within 3 to 7 business days after the final inspection.

### 6. Can I rent a car for one day?

Yes. Standard rentals can generally be booked for a minimum of 24 hours.

### 7. Can I rent a vehicle for several months?

Yes. Long-term rental arrangements are available subject to vehicle availability and contract approval.

### 8. Can I take the vehicle outside Lagos?

Yes, subject to the rental agreement and any applicable geographic restrictions.

### 9. Can I take the vehicle outside Nigeria?

Only with prior written authorization from JK Rentals.

### 10. Do your vehicles come with fuel?

Vehicles are provided with an agreed fuel level. Customers are generally expected to return them with the agreed level.

### 11. Can I request a chauffeur?

Yes. Chauffeur-driven services are available for selected vehicles and bookings.

### 12. Can JK Rentals deliver the vehicle?

Vehicle delivery is available in selected locations and may attract a delivery fee.

### 13. What happens if I return the car late?

Late returns may attract additional charges. Customers should contact JK Rentals before the scheduled return time if they require an extension.

### 14. What happens if the vehicle breaks down?

Contact JK Rentals immediately. Our support team will coordinate appropriate assistance depending on the situation.

### 15. What should I do after an accident?

Prioritize safety, contact the appropriate authorities where necessary and notify JK Rentals as soon as possible.

### 16. Can I use the rental car for ride-hailing?

Only if the vehicle has been specifically authorized for such use under a written agreement. Standard rentals do not automatically permit commercial passenger transportation.

### 17. Can I use the vehicle for a wedding?

Yes. JK Rentals offers event and wedding vehicle rentals, including selected premium vehicles and chauffeur services.

### 18. Can I add another driver?

Yes, provided the additional driver meets the company's eligibility requirements and is formally approved.

### 19. Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancellation is permitted according to the cancellation terms associated with the booking.

### 20. Can I change my pickup date?

Usually, yes. Changes are subject to availability and may affect pricing.

### 21. Do you provide airport pickup?

Yes. Airport transfer and vehicle delivery services may be arranged in advance.

### 22. What documents do I need?

Depending on the rental type, you may need a valid driving licence, government-issued identification, contact information and other verification documents.

### 23. What if I lose the vehicle keys?

Contact JK Rentals immediately. Replacement and recovery costs may be charged to the renter.

### 24. What happens if I receive a traffic ticket?

The customer is generally responsible for traffic violations and associated penalties incurred during the rental period.

### 25. Can I smoke inside the vehicle?

Smoking inside JK Rentals vehicles is prohibited unless specifically authorized.

### 26. Can I bring pets?

Pets may be permitted in selected vehicles subject to prior approval and appropriate cleaning requirements.

### 27. What happens if I damage the vehicle?

The incident will be documented and assessed according to the rental agreement and applicable protection terms.

### 28. Can I choose a specific vehicle?

Customers may request a specific vehicle, but availability is not guaranteed until confirmed by JK Rentals.

### 29. Do you offer corporate discounts?

Corporate customers may qualify for customized rates based on rental volume, duration and agreement terms.

### 30. How do I contact JK Rentals?
